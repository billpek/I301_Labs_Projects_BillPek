Sweetwater Tavern

A Southwestern-themed brewpub from the Great American Restaurants group, with beer brewed in-house, a patio, and a big menu spanning beers, Tex-Mex eggrolls, chicken tenders, juicy burgers, salmon, and prime rib. Everyone leaves Sweetwater happy.

8.5/10

The vibe

Sweetwater has a cozy, home-style American restaurant atmosphere, and it brews its own beer on site. It works for just about anything:

  • Date night
  • Dinner with family or friends
  • Special occasions
  • A quick, casual lunch

I've been to the Centreville and Sterling locations. There's also one in Falls Church (Merrifield) that I haven't tried yet.

What to order

My go-to plate is the chicken tenders with fries and coleslaw — the one in the photo above.

“The Swiss army knife of sit-down American restaurants.”

The verdict: 8.5/10

A real do-it-all restaurant. The food never disappoints, and there isn't an occasion where a trip to Sweetwater wouldn't be fitting.

Quick facts

  • Cuisine: Southwestern & American
  • Known for: House-brewed beer
  • Price: $$
  • Best for: Any occasion
  • Centreville: 14250 Sweetwater Ln, Centreville, VA 20121
  • Sterling: 45980 Waterview Plaza
  • Merrifield: 3066 Gate House Plaza, Falls Church
