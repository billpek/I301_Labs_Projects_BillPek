Downtown Leesburg

MELT Gourmet Cheeseburgers

A small burger counter on East Market Street with a multitude of craft burger choices, house-made buns, and even a create-your-own burger option.

9.5/10
  1. House-made bun
  2. Lettuce & tomato
  3. Bacon & red onion
  4. Bleu cheese
  5. Beef patty
  6. Bottom bun

The vibe

They cook everything in the open, so you get to see how fresh everything is. I firmly believe there's something for everyone here, including patties beyond beef:

  • Beef
  • Chicken
  • Turkey
  • Veggie

What to order

My go-to is the Bacon-Bleu Burger — built layer by layer next to the photo above.

“Not just the best burger in Northern Virginia, this place will give any burger spot a run for its money.”

The verdict: 9.5/10

Anyone who enjoys burgers should be coming here. With so many craft burger choices and a create-your-own option, there's something for everyone.

Quick facts

  • Cuisine: Burgers
  • Known for: Craft burgers on house-made buns
  • Price: $$
  • Best for: Anyone who loves burgers
  • Leesburg: 525 E Market St, Suite J, Leesburg, VA 20176
