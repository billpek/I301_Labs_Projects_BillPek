Centreville · Afghan

Kabob Express

An order-at-the-counter Afghan kabob spot in Centreville. For the price and the amount of food you get, this is a great spot for anyone who wants a good, authentic meal.

9/10

The vibe

Authentic kabobs, a great atmosphere, and friendly staff. You order at the counter or kiosk, and one tray covers it all:

  • Kabobs fresh off the grill
  • White rice and seasoned rice
  • Side salad
  • Unlimited sides
  • A large side of naan

What to order

My go-to is the bone-in chicken platter. They have unlimited sides, so I usually go for the beef and potato soup or a side of hummus.

“Authentic kabobs, great atmosphere, friendly staff, never not worth it.”

The verdict: 9/10

For the price and the amount of food you get, this is a great spot for anyone who wants an introduction to kabobs or just wants a good, authentic meal.

Quick facts

  • Cuisine: Afghan
  • Known for: Kabobs & unlimited sides
  • Price: $$
