Fair Oaks Mall · Fairfax

Sushi On

Nestled in the corner of Fair Oaks Mall, Sushi On is a hidden gem that isn't as popular as other choices, but it's guaranteed to leave you with a full belly and a positive impression.

9/10

My go-to: sashimi salad with assorted rolls

The vibe

It's an all-you-can-eat buffet, so knock yourself out with all of their sushi options. The spread goes way beyond sushi, too:

  • Sushi and sashimi
  • Crab and lobster (seasonal)
  • Hot sides
  • Warm meats like bulgogi, pork, and chicken
  • Respectable dessert options

It's inside the mall, so the Fair Oaks Mall garage is the easiest place to park.

What to order

My go-to plate is the sashimi salad with assorted sushi rolls — the one in the photo above.

“Fresh, all-you-can-eat sushi, plus so much more.”

The verdict: 9/10

If you want fresh, all-you-can-eat sushi plus more authentic Japanese-style food and dessert, this is the spot to come to.

Quick facts

  • Cuisine: Japanese & Korean
  • Known for: All-you-can-eat sushi
  • Price: $$
  • Best for: A big appetite
  • Fairfax: 11717-L Fair Oaks Mall, Fairfax, VA 22033
