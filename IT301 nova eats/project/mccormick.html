Crystal City · National Harbor

McCormick & Schmick's

A more upscale seafood and steak house, with fish and shellfish from the Pacific, the Atlantic, and the Gulf alongside aged steaks. It's more expensive, but the quality of the steak is fantastic.

9/10

The vibe

A classy, sit-down steak house that's made for a night out:

  • Birthdays and celebrations
  • Special occasions
  • When you're in the mood for a top-quality cut of steak

I came here to celebrate my 14th and 21st birthday, plus my first internship, so it holds a lot of sentimental value for me.

What to order

My go-to is the 14 oz New York strip with mashed potatoes and asparagus — the one in the photo above.
